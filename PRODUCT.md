# Purpose

KyleHub introduces Kyle's projects and hosts the German and English legal pages
for services under `kylehub.dev` and `porvi.de`.

Visitors need to find a project's repository or identify the operator of a
service. Keep the introduction short, put projects on the homepage, and make
legal pages reachable from every page.

The site is static. Project selection is manual; repository metadata updates
when the site builds. Keep navigation usable by keyboard and legal text readable
at 200% zoom and on narrow screens. Language links must lead to an existing
translation.

Use first-person copy for the project introduction. Legal texts describe the
operator and actual service behavior; their wording needs a separate factual
review when hosting or data processing changes.
