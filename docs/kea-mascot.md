# Kea mascot direction

Recorded 7 September 2026 from Kyle's brand notes.

Kea is the preferred animal for Kyle's personal brand and a possible mascot for
the KyleHub website. The visual treatment is still open. This note records the
direction for later design work; it does not replace the current identity.

## Character

The connection is curiosity through tinkering: notice something, investigate it,
take it apart and learn how it works. The shared K in Kyle and kea provides a
simple name connection.

Aim for a clever, alert, investigative bird with a slightly mischievous expression.
Playfulness should come from what it is doing, such as inspecting or manipulating
an object. Keep it confident and capable rather than clumsy or childish.

"Smart menace" is useful internal shorthand for the expression. Public copy
should describe curiosity, building and experimentation. Avoid turning the mascot
into a diagnosis-based joke or making destruction its whole personality.

## Visual directions to explore

| Direction | Idea | Potential use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark with mascot | Keep Kyle or KyleHub primary, with a kea beside, below or perched on the lettering. | Website introduction and personal identity. |
| K emblem | Explore a K with recognizable kea features, such as a hooked beak, head profile or perch. Keep the letter legible. | Avatar, favicon or compact mark. |
| Full mascot and name | A stylized kea leaning into or interacting with the name. | Larger illustrations and a more expressive name lockup. |

Integrate the bird with the composition so it feels intentional. Study real kea
references before drawing; the result should remain recognizable as a kea rather
than a generic parrot. Check compact marks at favicon size and in one color.

## Possible website adoption

Start by trying the mascot beside the existing wordmark or introduction. Follow
the current [design direction](../DESIGN.md) for typography, spacing and restrained
color. Keep project navigation and legal pages easy to read.

The first exploration should compare a small supporting mascot with a more
integrated name lockup. Replacing the shield, favicon or existing illustration is
a separate choice after those options have been reviewed. A new mascot does not
require a site redesign.

## Copy ideas, not selected taglines

- Building, breaking, iterating.
- Curiosity-driven work.
- Experiments and projects.
- Controlled chaos.
- Creative problem solving.

Prefer concrete descriptions in the finished site. These phrases are starting
points for exploration, not required homepage copy.
